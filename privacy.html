Privacy Policy

Pixtidy does not collect any data.

All photo analysis (finding duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and documents, blurry photos and large videos) happens entirely on your device. Your photos, videos and the results of the analysis are never uploaded, shared or sent to us or to any third party.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the network for its own features; it only connects to Apple when you make or restore a purchase through the App Store, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

The app needs access to your photo library to work. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for 30 days.

The app stores a small amount of information on your device only (such as cached analysis results and your choices of which photos to keep). Deleting the app removes it.

隐私政策

相册清理不收集任何数据。

所有照片分析（查找重复、相似、截图与文档、可能模糊的照片和大视频）都只在你的手机上完成。你的照片、视频以及分析结果不会上传、分享或发送给我们或任何第三方。

App 没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告、不做追踪。App 的功能不使用网络；只有在你通过 App Store 购买或恢复购买时才会连接 Apple，这部分由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

App 需要访问照片图库才能工作，你可以随时在“设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 照片”中更改。删除的照片会进入“照片”App 的“最近删除”，30 天内可以恢复。

App 只在你的手机上保存少量信息（例如分析结果缓存、你选择保留哪些照片），删除 App 即一并删除。
