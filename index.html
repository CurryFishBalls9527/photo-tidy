Support

Tidy finds duplicate, similar, blurry and screenshot-like photos and helps you free up space. Everything runs on your iPhone; no data is collected.

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and can be recovered for 30 days. Space is freed once you empty Recently Deleted (Photos › Utilities › Recently Deleted).

Restore Pro: open Tidy › Settings › Restore Purchases.

Questions or feedback: chaosstudio599@gmail.com · Privacy Policy

支持

相册清理帮你找出重复、相似、模糊的照片以及截图和文档，释放手机空间。所有处理都在手机上完成，不收集任何数据。

删除的照片会进入“照片”App 的“最近删除”，30 天内可以恢复；清空“最近删除”（照片 › 实用工具 › 最近删除）后才会真正腾出空间。

恢复 Pro：打开相册清理 › 设置 › 恢复购买。
